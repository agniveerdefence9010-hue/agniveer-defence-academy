Who We Are

A Mission-Driven Academy for Future Soldiers

Agniveer Defence Academy is dedicated to preparing young men and women for a career in India's Armed Forces. We combine professional athletic coaching, structured physical training and focused written-exam preparation under one roof.

Every cadet who joins us receives free hostel, free education, free food and free access to our training ground — because we believe talent and grit should never be held back by cost.

  • Expert, nationally-certified coaching
  • Daily physical + written exam preparation
  • Disciplined residential environment
  • Personal mentoring by the Director
